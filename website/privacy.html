Template notice

This Privacy Policy is a general template for Nemis by Nemorax and should be reviewed before an official commercial launch. It does not claim that every legal, operational, or registration requirement has already been completed.

1. Overview

Nemis is an AI-powered application developed and operated by Nemorax. This Policy explains the basic personal information that may be handled when you use Nemis.

2. Privacy Principles

Nemorax aims to handle personal information responsibly and in line with applicable Philippine data privacy principles, including transparency, legitimate purpose, and proportionality.

3. Information Nemis May Collect

  • Account information, such as email address and optional display name.
  • Login and session-related information needed to provide account access.
  • Conversation messages and history, if persistent chat history is enabled.
  • User settings, such as theme or welcome-screen preferences.
  • Feedback messages and support details you choose to submit.
  • Basic technical information needed to keep the service running, secure, and reliable.

4. Why Information May Be Used

Information may be used to provide Nemis features, respond to user messages, manage accounts, save settings, retain chat history where enabled, process feedback, troubleshoot service issues, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.

5. AI Processing

Messages sent to Nemis may be processed by AI/model services used by the deployment. Avoid submitting sensitive personal information, confidential school records, passwords, payment information, or other private data unless a feature explicitly requires it and an appropriate legal basis or consent exists.

6. Data Users Should Not Submit

Do not submit passwords, API keys, private tokens, bank or card details, government ID numbers, highly sensitive personal data, or private information about other people unless the feature clearly requires it and proper consent or legal basis exists.

7. How Information Is Protected

Nemorax should use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the Nemis deployment, such as access controls, secure configuration, limited access to stored data, and careful handling of credentials. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Retention and Deletion

Information should be kept only as long as needed for the service purpose, operational needs, legal requirements, or legitimate project records. Users may request correction or deletion where applicable, subject to identity verification, technical limits, legal obligations, and legitimate retention needs.

9. User Rights and Requests

Where applicable under Philippine data privacy law, users may ask about personal information handled through Nemis and may request access, correction, deletion, or other appropriate action. Requests should be sent through the contact details below.

10. Third-Party Services

Nemis may rely on third-party services selected or configured by Nemorax, such as hosting providers, database providers, AI/model providers, release platforms, security tools, or analytics tools if enabled. These services may process data according to their own terms and privacy practices.

11. Children, Students, and School Use

If Nemis is used by students or minors, use should be guided by appropriate parent, guardian, school, or teacher supervision. Users should not submit private information about themselves or others unless it is necessary for the feature and properly authorized.

12. Changes to This Policy

Nemorax may update this Policy as Nemis features, deployment setup, or legal requirements change. Continued use after an update means the updated Policy applies.
